## 📏 Running Shoe Size Guide

For running shoes, many runners prefer slightly more room than casual shoes.

General recommendation:

- Leave approximately **0.5–1 cm of space** between your longest toe and the front of the shoe.
- Your heel should remain secure.
- Your toes should be able to move comfortably.
- Try shoes with the socks you normally use for running.

> Fit can vary depending on the brand and shoe model.
